Questions

Everything you need before the drop.

How do I order?

Use the preorder form at the bottom of this page. Fill in your name, contact, pack, flavor preference, and pickup window. Then copy your order summary and send it via Cash App, text, or email. We confirm every order personally.

When is pickup?

Current pickup windows are Friday 5–7 PM and Saturday 12–2 PM. Event and catering orders have separate pickup or delivery arrangements. Join the Crown Club for exact location announcements.

Do you cater events?

Yes. The Creamy Queen offers 50-cup party drops starting at $325 and 100-cup event drops starting at $650. We also do branded pop-up appearances for markets, schools, churches, gyms, and corporate events. Use the preorder form or email us directly.

How long do cups last?

The 8 oz cups stay fresh in the freezer for up to two weeks. Best enjoyed within the first 3–4 days for peak flavor and texture. Keep lids sealed until serving.

Do you offer dairy-free options?

Not yet — but dairy-free and vegan crowns are on the roadmap. Join the Crown Club to be first to know when dairy-free drops launch.

What allergens are included?

Current flavors contain milk and may contain wheat/gluten (cookie crumble). Please contact us before ordering if you have specific allergen concerns. We are happy to discuss options for your situation.

Is there a loyalty program?

Yes — the Crown Club. Join via the Drop Alert section above. Buy 8 cups across any drops and receive one Crown Cup free. Crown Club members also get early drop access, birthday rewards, and catering discounts.
